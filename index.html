Why are we doing this?

Movember is the leading charity changing the face of men's health, and this Movember we're joining them in memory of Maxi.

Who was Maxi?

Maxi (2007 - 2025) was a friend of mine who unfortunatley lost his battle with mental health in November of last year. This Movember we remember what a great man he was and try to make sure this doesn't happen to anyone else.
